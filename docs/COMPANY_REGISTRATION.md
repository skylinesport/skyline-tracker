# Registering Skylinesports Pvt Ltd

A working checklist + cost guide to incorporate as a **Private Limited Company** in India.

**Why Pvt Ltd:** you want to **raise investment** — investors buy *shares*, and only a Private Limited Company can issue them. (An LLP can't, so it's off the table.)

> ⚠️ Not legal/tax advice. A CA/CS files this for you. Costs are approximate (2026) and vary by state and service.

---

## 1 · Documents from each founder
Both **Ayush** and **Pooja** provide a full set. Scan as clear PDF/JPG, named per person.

- [ ] **PAN card** — each
- [ ] **Aadhaar card** — each
- [ ] **One more ID proof** (Voter ID / Passport / Driving Licence) — each
- [ ] **Address proof in your own name** — bank statement or utility bill, **≤ 2 months old** — each
- [ ] **Passport-size photo** — each
- [ ] **Email + mobile** (Aadhaar-linked mobile preferred; needed for OTP + DSC video KYC) — each

## 2 · Registered office address
A founder's home works fine — you just need proof + the owner's permission.

- [ ] **Latest utility bill** of the premises (electricity/gas/water/phone), **≤ 2 months old**
- [ ] **NOC** from the property owner (simple "no objection" letter to use the address)
- [ ] **Rent / lease agreement** — only if the premises is rented

## 3 · Decisions to finalize
No documents, but the filing needs these settled.

- [ ] **Company name + 1 backup** — e.g. "Skylinesports Private Limited" + a one-line meaning
- [ ] **Equity split** — Ayush % / Pooja % (becomes your shareholding; agree it clearly)
- [ ] **Authorized & paid-up capital** — nominal is fine, e.g. ₹1,00,000 authorized (not deposited upfront)
- [ ] **Business objects** — what the company does (esports tournaments, gaming & social platform, software)
- [ ] **Founder vesting** (recommended) — investors expect founders' shares to vest over ~4 years

## 4 · Handled by the CA / service
You just e-sign these.

- [ ] **DSC** (Digital Signature) for both directors
- [ ] **DIN, eMOA/eAOA, DIR-2, INC-9** — drafted & auto-generated
- [ ] **AGILE-PRO** — GST, EPFO/ESIC, bank account, professional tax

## Approximate cost
Ballpark for a 2-director Pvt Ltd — varies by state (stamp duty) and service.

| Item | Approx cost |
|---|---|
| DSC — 2 directors | ₹2,000 – ₹4,000 |
| Govt / MCA fee (capital ≤ ₹15L) | ≈ ₹0 * |
| Stamp duty on MOA/AOA (state-wise) | ₹500 – ₹2,000+ |
| Name reservation + PAN + TAN | ~₹1,200 |
| Professional / service fee | ₹3,000 – ₹10,000 |
| **One-time to incorporate** | **₹6,000 – ₹15,000** |

\* Incorporation form fee is waived for small authorized capital; you still pay stamp duty + professional fees.

**Also budget for recurring & related costs:**
- **Annual compliance** (audit + ROC filings + ITR + CA): **₹15,000 – ₹30,000 / year**
- **Apple Developer** (Organization): **$99/yr** (~₹8,300) · **Google Play**: **$25** one-time
- **D-U-N-S number** (for Apple Org): **free** · **Business bank account / GST**: mostly free to set up

**Quick read:** ~**₹8–15k to incorporate**, then ~**₹15–30k/year** to stay compliant.

## How it happens
All through the govt **MCA portal** via the integrated **SPICe+** form. ~7–15 working days.

1. **Gather docs + settle decisions** (sections 1–3). *Slow part is usually a ≤2-month-old address proof.*
2. **Get DSCs** for both directors (quick video KYC each).
3. **Reserve the company name** (SPICe+ Part A) — MCA checks it's unique + not trademark-clashing.
4. **File SPICe+ Part B** — company details, directors, capital, office, MOA/AOA, AGILE-PRO.
5. **MCA issues the Certificate of Incorporation** — with CIN, PAN & TAN. You're a company. 🎉
6. **After:** open a current bank account, register GST (when needed), appoint an auditor, then get the **D-U-N-S → Apple Organization** account.

**Easiest route:** use a startup-friendly service — **Razorpay Rize**, **IndiaFilings**, **ClearTax**, or **Zolvit** — upload the docs above and they run the whole SPICe+ filing. Or DIY on [mca.gov.in](https://www.mca.gov.in).
